## Navigating the Soul's Remnant Wiki

If you are looking for the definitive resource for the game, the **Soul's Remnant wiki** is your primary destination for all information regarding this 2D platformer MMORPG. As a player-driven project, the wiki serves as a central hub for the community to document the exploration-focused world and social mechanics that define the game. Whether you are a new player trying to understand the basics or a veteran looking for specific item data, the wiki is the most reliable place to start your research.

The wiki is hosted on the wiki.gg platform and provides a collaborative environment for players to share their findings. Since the project’s inception on April 15th, 2016, by developers Sage and Rajah, the game has grown into a vast experience. The wiki currently hosts over 1,100 articles, reflecting the depth of the world these developers have created.

## Understanding the Game’s Scope

*Soul's Remnant* is distinct for its focus on social interaction and exploration within a 2D platforming environment. Unlike many traditional MMORPGs that prioritize combat-heavy progression, this title encourages players to engage with the world and each other.

Because the game is constantly evolving, the wiki functions as a "continual work-in-progress." This means that information is added and verified by the community as updates are released. Players should keep in mind that the site is maintained by volunteers. If you notice missing information or incomplete pages, the best course of action is to contribute to the project rather than contacting the developers directly. The developers, Sage and Rajah, are focused on the game's evolution; therefore, the community asks that players do not ping them regarding wiki content gaps.
