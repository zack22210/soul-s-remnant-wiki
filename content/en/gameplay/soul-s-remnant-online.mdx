## What is Soul's Remnant Online?

If you are looking for information on **soul s remnant online**, you have likely discovered the charming and expansive world of *Soul's Remnant*. Currently available in Early Access, the game invites players to venture into a mysterious world filled with monsters, secrets, and dungeons, either solo or with friends.

## A Classless Approach to Character Building

One of the most defining aspects of *Soul's Remnant* is its commitment to player freedom through a classless skill system. Unlike traditional MMORPGs that lock you into a specific role or archetype, this game allows you to customize your character's abilities and stats to fit your preferred playstyle.

By utilizing various stats and a flexible skill set, you can tailor your character to handle different challenges. Whether you want to focus on raw damage, support, or a hybrid build, the system provides the tools necessary to experiment. This flexibility extends to your equipment as well; players can find and upgrade gear of various rarities, allowing for deeper customization as you progress through the game's many dungeons and boss encounters.

## Social Gameplay and Player-Driven Economy

At its core, *Soul's Remnant* is a social experience. The game is built to foster a community where players can interact, cooperate, and build together. Beyond simply grouping up for boss fights, the game offers several systems that emphasize the "MMO" aspect of the genre.

### Guilds and Cooperation
Players can form or join guilds, which offer various perks to help members grow stronger. This system encourages long-term collaboration and helps create a sense of belonging within the game world.

### The Economy and Housing
The game features a fully realized player-driven economy. This is supported by:
*   **Player Shops:** A venue for players to trade equipment, materials, and crafted items.
*   **Crafting and Gathering:** A robust system that allows players to unlock recipes and create their own items, which are essential for the economy.
*   **Housing:** A space for players to call their own, further integrating the social aspect into the fabric of the game.

### Interacting with Villagers
Beyond interacting with other players, you can befriend 10 unique villager NPCs. Each NPC has their own personality and story to share, adding depth to the world and making the environment feel more alive.
