## Core Gameplay Mechanics

One of the most defining aspects of *Soul's Remnant* is its commitment to player freedom. Unlike traditional MMORPGs that lock players into rigid roles, this game utilizes a classless skill system. This allows you to customize your character's abilities and stats to fit your preferred playstyle.

The journey in *Soul's Remnant* is not just about combat; it’s about becoming part of a living world. The game features 10 unique villager NPCs, each with their own personality and backstory.
