## From Passion Project to Steam Early Access

*Soul's Remnant* began in 2016 as a solo project led by Chaomoon. Over an eight-year period, the game was shaped by a series of public playtests that allowed a dedicated community to provide feedback, helping the title grow into the expansive 2D platformer MMORPG it is today. 

As of 2025, the team has expanded, with Rajah joining to assist in development and future updates. Because community feedback remains a core pillar of the design philosophy, the developers encourage players to get involved during this Early Access phase. If you want to contribute to the game's evolution, the best way to participate is by playing the current version on Steam and sharing your experiences with the development team.

## What to Expect in Soul's Remnant

*Soul's Remnant* is a free-to-play, 2D platformer MMORPG that focuses on social interaction, exploration, and character customization. The game features a vibrant, colorful pixel art style that brings its large world to life. Whether you are a solo player or looking to team up, the game offers several systems designed to encourage group play:

*   **Classless Skill System:** You are not locked into a specific archetype. Instead, you can customize your skills and utilize various stats to build your character exactly how you want.
*   **Social Gameplay:** Beyond simple grouping, the game includes a guild system with specific perks, player-driven housing, and a player-run economy supported by player shops.
*   **Combat and Exploration:** Players can explore a large world filled with secrets, dungeons, and challenging bosses. The game also features a comprehensive quest system and a crafting and gathering loop.
*   **Immersive World-Building:** You can befriend 10 unique villager NPCs, each with their own personality and story to uncover.
