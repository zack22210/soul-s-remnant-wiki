## What is Soul's Remnant?

*Soul's Remnant* is a free-to-play 2D platformer MMORPG that emphasizes social interaction, exploration, and player customization. Developed by Chaomoon, the game invites players into a vibrant, pixel-art world filled with secrets, dungeons, and challenging bosses.

Unlike traditional MMORPGs that lock you into specific roles, *Soul's Remnant* utilizes a classless skill system. This allows players to build their characters using a variety of stats and skills, offering a more flexible approach to combat and progression. Whether you prefer to focus on gathering and crafting or taking on high-level dungeons with friends, the game is designed to support a wide range of playstyles.

## Core Gameplay Features

The world of *Soul's Remnant* is built around a blend of cooperative play and a player-driven economy. If you are preparing to jump in, here are the key systems you should be aware of:

*   **Classless Progression:** Customize your character using a flexible skill and stat system rather than being restricted to a pre-defined class.
*   **Social & Party Play:** The game encourages teaming up to defeat monsters and bosses. There is also a robust guild system that offers specific perks for members.
*   **Player-Driven Economy:** Engage in player shops, housing, and party grinding to interact with the world and other players.
*   **Crafting and Gathering:** A full system is in place for collecting materials and crafting items, with numerous recipes to discover.
*   **Exploration:** The game features a large world with secrets, dungeons, and environmental hazards—including a day-night cycle that changes the atmosphere of your journey.
