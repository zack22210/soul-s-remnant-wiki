## What to Expect in Soul's Remnant Playtest Gameplay

The gameplay of *Soul's Remnant* is designed around exploration, platforming, and social interaction. Unlike traditional, rigid MMORPGs, it utilizes a 2D side-scrolling perspective with a colorful, cute pixel art style. 

During public playtests, players have been able to experience the core loop of the game: exploring a large world, fighting monsters, conquering dungeons, and gathering materials. The game also features a dynamic day-and-night cycle. Players must remain vigilant, as the world becomes significantly more dangerous when night falls. 

Because the game is in Early Access, the gameplay continues to evolve based on community feedback. The development team actively encourages players to join their community channels to share experiences and shape future updates.

## Core Gameplay Mechanics and Features

*Soul's Remnant* offers a deep suite of RPG systems that allow for highly customized playstyles. The primary mechanics verified by the developers include:

### Classless Skill System and Stats
Instead of locking players into traditional archetypes like warrior, mage, or rogue, the game features a classless skill system. You can customize your skills and allocate various stats freely, allowing you to build a character that perfectly matches your preferred playstyle.

### Equipment and Progression
As you defeat monsters and bosses, you will find equipment of various rarities. Players can upgrade this gear to tackle more challenging content.

### Crafting and Gathering
For players who enjoy non-combat activities, *Soul's Remnant* features a comprehensive crafting and gathering system. You can explore the world to gather resources, unlock many different recipes, and craft useful items.

### Questing and NPC Relationships
The game features a comprehensive quest system with fun missions that reward progression. Additionally, you can befriend 10 unique villager NPCs. Each NPC has their own distinct personality and backstory for you to uncover as you build your relationship with them.

## Social Systems and Player-Driven Economy

A major focus of *Soul's Remnant* is its social and cooperative gameplay. The developer designed the game to be played with friends or cooperative partners met along the journey.

*   **Parties and Party Grinding:** Team up with other players to defeat monsters, share experience, and grind for rare drops.
*   **Challenging Dungeons and Bosses:** Coordinate with your party to conquer difficult instanced dungeons and powerful bosses.
*   **Guild System:** Join or create a guild to unlock various perks and benefits for you and your guildmates.
*   **Player-Driven Economy:** Set up player shops to trade items with the community, fostering a fully player-run marketplace.
*   **Housing:** Personalize your own space within the game world using the player housing system.

## Development History and Community Origins

The journey of *Soul's Remnant* is deeply tied to its player community. For eight years—from 2016 to 2024—the game was developed entirely by a single creator, Chaomoon, as a dedicated passion project. 

During this solo development phase, Chaomoon hosted public playtests. These playtests allowed a dedicated community of players to form, test early builds, and provide critical feedback. The success of these playtests helped the game grow into a much larger project. 

To support this growth, the development team expanded in 2025 when Rajah joined Chaomoon to assist with future updates and ongoing development. Additionally, the game features a massive, original soundtrack of over 40 songs, all written, composed, and produced by Chaomoon.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Soul's Remnant free to play?
Yes. *Soul's Remnant* is a free-to-play 2D platformer MMORPG. You can access the game and participate in its development journey through Steam Early Access.

### How does the class system work in Soul's Remnant?
The game does not use traditional character classes. Instead, it features a classless skill system and customizable stats, allowing you to build and modify your character's abilities freely.

### Can I play Soul's Remnant solo?
While you can explore the world and complete quests on your own, the game is designed with a heavy focus on social gameplay. Many features, such as challenging dungeons, bosses, party grinding, guilds, and the player-driven economy, are built to be experienced with others.
